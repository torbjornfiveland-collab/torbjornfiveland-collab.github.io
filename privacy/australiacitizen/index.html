Privacy policy – Australia Citizenship Practice

Fiveland Studio · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Australia Citizenship Practice is a practice app from Fiveland Studio. The app does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information about you.

What is stored

The name you may enter, your language choices, your test date and your practice progress are stored only in the app on your device. We do not have access to this data.

Network, purchases and third parties

The app itself sends no data about you or your practice. The purchase of the full version is handled by Google Play, which uses the network when you buy and when the app checks the purchase. Google Play's billing library, which is part of the app, may send technical diagnostic information to Google. Google's privacy policy and terms apply to this. We do not learn who you are and we do not see your payment details. The app uses no analytics and no advertising.

When you tap a source link, this privacy policy or "More apps", it opens in your browser or in Google Play, and their own terms apply.

Report an error

The "Report an error" button opens your email app with a pre-filled email to fivelandstudio@gmail.com. It contains the app's name and version, the language you use, the question it concerns and your device model. The app sends nothing by itself; you see the email and decide whether to send it. If you send it, we use it only to fix the error and to reply to you.

Exporting progress

When you export a backup under Me, a file is saved on your device in a place you choose. The file does not leave your device unless you share it yourself.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup in Android, the app's local data may be included in the backup in your Google account, according to your own settings.

Deleting your data

Go to Me → Reset → Start completely over, or uninstall the app. Your purchase is kept by Google Play.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the Australian citizenship test.
